Rate & Currency

Your house rules

  • Seen opponents pay the configured Seen points. Unseen opponents pay the configured Unseen points.
  • A player who viewed the joker through Dublee pays no winning points. Maal settlement still applies.
  • A Dublee finisher receives the configured bonus (default 5) in their Maal before settlement. Enter only their original Maal; the calculator adds the bonus once.
  • A foul player pays Maal settlement, but no winning points. The separate foul penalty goes to the following round’s winner. If the offender wins that round, their own penalty cancels out.
  • Players on Hold do not participate in that round.

Dublee bonuses and previous-round foul adjustments are automatic. Foul points are captured under the originating round’s rules and converted using the receiving round’s rate. Final settlement waits if the last round has an unresolved foul.
